About me

Hi, I'm Jay. The Finance Scientist.

Finance professional, finance educator and business consultant, focused on making finance simple and AI-enabled.

01 · My story

Why "The Finance Scientist"?

A scientist doesn't guess. They observe, test and let the evidence decide. That's how I approach finance, whether it's a company's month-end close or someone's first budget.

I've spent 7+ years in finance across India and the UAE. I worked as a Finance Controller for a Dubai entity, and today I'm a Financial Analyst at Neotas in Bengaluru.

Along the way I got hooked on one question: how much of finance's busywork can AI take off our plates? The answer turned out to be: a lot. So I started testing, building and sharing.

What I believe

Finance should be simple. If it can't be explained to a friend over chai, it hasn't been understood yet.

AI is a tool, not a replacement. Let AI do the grind and let humans do the thinking.

Evidence over opinions. I only share what I've tested, and I use verifiable sources.
