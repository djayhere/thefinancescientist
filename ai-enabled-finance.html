My top focus

AI-enabled finance: let AI do the grind, humans do the thinking.

This is my core focus. I explore how AI can make finance work faster, more accurate and more useful, and I share what works in plain language.

01 · Why it matters

Finance teams spend too much time on work a machine could do.

Matching transactions. Reformatting reports. Chasing explanations for variances. Reading long documents for one number. This is the work that fills a finance professional's week.

AI can now handle a large share of it, with a human checking the output. That frees time for what finance is really for: judgement, advice and better decisions.

I'm not selling hype. I'm testing these tools on real finance problems and sharing what works, what doesn't, and where you still need to be careful.

"AI won't replace finance professionals. Finance professionals who use AI will replace those who don't."

03 · My approach

The Finance Scientist method.

Four steps I apply to every AI finance experiment.

01 · Observe

Find the grind

Spot the repetitive, rules-based work that eats hours.

02 · Hypothesise

Pick the right tool

Choose the simplest AI approach that could solve it.

03 · Test

Run it on real data

Compare results against the manual way and measure time saved.

04 · Conclude

Keep a human in the loop

Document controls, review points and what to never automate.
